FireNomics

I write FireNomics, a set of practical FIRE guides built on arithmetic you can re-run: savings rate, withdrawal strategy, index funds, and where the 4% rule stops holding. Every guide works the numbers first and lets the conclusion land where it lands.

Why 50-year retirements stress the 4% rule

The 4% rule was derived from 30-year retirement windows. Retiring near 35 can mean funding 50 years, and the failure modes shift: sequence-of-returns risk bites hardest in the first decade, when a bad market year forces withdrawals out of a shrunken portfolio. The guides on the site run survival checks across those long windows instead of quoting one safe number.

Price decisions like an investor

The same math prices everyday choices. A $997 course has an expected value you can hold against fifteen years of index returns. A desired salary has a savings rate behind it that shows whether it funds the life attached to it. Working the arithmetic first keeps the plan honest when the market misbehaves.
